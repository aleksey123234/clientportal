SECTION A - FA PACKAGES (Final Application)
===========================================================================

---

SNIPPET 1
Title:
FA_CR - Documents to sign (RLS, IMM1, IMM2, Personal Statement)

Content:
The Final Application for Criminal Rehabilitation includes 4 documents to sign and mail back.

RLS form: Print it. Go to page 4. Sign at the bottom of page 4. Do NOT write the date - leave the date blank.

IMM 1 form: Print ALL pages. Sign in Section 21, which is under the employment history section.

IMM 2 form: Only if included in the email. Print all pages. Sign at the bottom of each page.

Personal Statement: Print it. Sign the last page above the client's name.

All documents must be properly printed, signed, and mailed - not emailed.

---

SNIPPET 2
Title:
FA_CR - Documents the client must gather themselves

Content:
In addition to the signed documents, the client must gather these items themselves and include them in the envelope:

1. Two passport-size photos. Must be taken within the last 2 months. Size: 2 inches wide by 2 and three-quarter inches tall.

2. Copy of ID with photo. Best option: copy of passport (photo page). If no passport: copy of Birth Certificate AND copy of Driver's Licence - both required together. One alone is not enough.

3. Reference letters. Minimum 2 letters. Only 1 can be from a family member. Best: letters from professionals such as a doctor, counselor, parole officer, or educator.

4. Original State Searches from the beginning of the application.

5. One original fingerprint card, same as done at the start of the application.

SNIPPET 4
Title:
FA_CR - Complete checklist of everything to send

Content:
The envelope must contain all of the following.

From the email - documents to sign:

- RLS form, signed on page 4, no date
- IMM 1 form, signed in section 21
- IMM 2 form, signed (only if received in email)
- Personal Statement, signed on last page above client's name

Documents the client gathers themselves:

- 2 passport-size photos taken within last 2 months
- Copy of passport, OR Birth Certificate plus Driver's Licence together
- Minimum 2 reference letters, maximum 1 from family
- Original State Searches from the beginning of the application
- One original fingerprint card, same as done at the start

Optional - not required:

- Employment letters, certificates, community involvement proof, therapy evidence, rehabilitation program completion

SNIPPET 5
Title:
FA_CR and FA_TRP - Are additional documents required?

Content:
No. Additional documents are optional, not required.

The email listed optional documents such as employment letters, certificates, proof of community involvement, therapy evidence, or rehabilitation program completion.

The client does not need to send these. The application will not be delayed if they are not included.

If the client has them and wants to include them, they may.

---

SNIPPET 6
Title:
FA_TRP - Documents to sign and send by email

Content:
The Final Application package for TRP (Temporary Resident Permit) has 4 documents to sign and send back by email.

1. RLS form - sign on page 4. Date it as instructed.
2. IMM 1 form - print all pages, sign in Section 21 under the employment history section.
3. IMM 2 form - only if included. Sign at the bottom of each page.
4. Personal Statement - sign the last page above the client's name.

All documents must be properly scanned. Photos of documents are not accepted.

The client must also include:

- 2 passport-size photos taken within the last 2 months. Digital file from photographer is preferred.
- Copy of passport (photo page with personal info).
- Proof of urgent travel: plane tickets, hotel reservations, event tickets, letter from employer, or any document proving the need to enter Canada.

Send everything by email to FA@pardonsandwaivers.com. Add URGENT to the subject line.

SNIPPET 7
Title:
SERVICE - Forms to fill out and send back

Content:
The Service Package has 4 forms the client must complete and send back.

1. Consent Form - sign and date it.
2. Credit Card Authorization Form - fill in payment details and sign.
3. Fingerprint Instructions - read carefully. Follow the instructions to get fingerprints done at a local police station or approved provider. Bring the instruction sheet with you.
4. Client Information Form - the most important form. The client must list every address they have lived at and every place they have worked, with no gaps in time. Even short jobs must be included. Missing time periods will cause delays.

All forms must be returned by email to FA@pardonsandwaivers.com. Add the word URGENT to the subject line.

URGENT - Travel documents needed for urgent TRP

Content:
For urgent TRP (Temporary Resident Permit) travel, the client needs to provide proof that they are travelling to Canada.

Acceptable documents include any of the following:

- Plane ticket or flight itinerary
- Hotel reservation
- Tickets to an event in Canada

All documents must be scanned properly and sent by email to FA@pardonsandwaivers.com. Add URGENT to the subject line.

Optional: employment letters, certificates, therapy evidence - not required.

SNIPPET 14
Title:
CIF_INCOMPLETE - Client Information Form has missing information

Content:
We reviewed the Client Information Form the client sent us and some information is missing.

We need all addresses and employment history since the client was 18 years old, with no gaps.

For addresses: the client must provide at minimum the city, province or state, and country - and the years they lived there. Example: Toronto, Ontario, Canada - 2010 to 2015. No gaps are allowed between dates.

For employment: the client must list every job, even short ones. Example: McDonald's 2017 to 2018, then next job 2018 to 2020. No gaps.

The client can skip months and just provide years. But there must be no missing time periods between entries.

The client must send the corrected information to us by email: forms@pardonsandwaivers.com, or by mail.

AFFIDAVIT - Forms to sign, notarize, and mail back

Content:
We sent the client an Affidavit package. This is a document that needs to be signed and notarized.

There are two types of affidavit:

1. Affidavit confirming no information regarding payment of a fine from the court.
2. Affidavit confirming the victim's age.

The client must:

1. Read the instructions in the attached letter carefully.
2. Sign the affidavit form.
3. Get the form notarized by a Notary Public or Commissioner of Oaths.
4. Mail the notarized forms back to us as soon as possible.

This package must be returned by mail. Do not email affidavit forms.

We cannot proceed with the application without the notarized affidavit.

SNIPPET 22
Title:
FP_CONVERSION - RCMP digital fingerprint conversion required

Content:
The RCMP no longer accepts traditional ink-and-roll fingerprint cards. All fingerprints must now be submitted electronically through an RCMP-accredited fingerprinting agency. The cost for fingerprint conversion is $200.

The client must mail us the following:

1. Two original ink-and-roll fingerprint cards - taken on RCMP C-216C form (legal size 8.5 x 14 inches) or FBI FD-258 card. Can be taken at a local police station, fingerprinting agency, or notary public. The card must be signed by both the client and the official taking the fingerprints. Unsigned cards are invalid.

2. RCMP Form - all boxes must be checked, signed with a physical wet signature.

3. Completed Card-Scan Application Form - attached to the email, must be filled out in full.

4. Two signed Consent Forms - attached to the email.

5. Photocopy of two valid government-issued IDs - one must be a photo ID. If IDs are not in English or French, they must be translated by an authorized translation agency.

Mail everything to our office. If the client has questions, they should call client care at 1-800-543-2137 extension 823.

CCR_ONE_OF_TWO - Received only one CCR out of two needed

We need to confirm if the client took two separate sets of fingerprints - one for the US Waiver and one for the Record Suspension (Pardon) application.

If the client believes they did take two sets, they should let us know right away and we will follow up with the RCMP.

SNIPPET 28
Title:
FBI_UPDATE - New fingerprints needed for updated State Background Check and FBI

Content:
For the final application we need new State background checks and an updated FBI report. Background checks and FBI reports cannot be older than one year - older documents will be returned by the consulate.

The client has two options:

Option 1: Request the searches themselves at the fingerprinting agency. This is faster. The client can mail us the results directly instead of the fingerprint cards.

Option 2: Mail us the fingerprint cards and we will request the searches on the client's behalf. The client must take prints the same way as at the beginning - on FD-258 cards - and mail 2 fingerprint cards to us along with the completed and signed release form (attached to the email).
